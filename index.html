Postdoctoral researcher in Earth System Science at UC Irvine, working with the Climate, Pollution, and Solutions group. I study regional climate dynamics, heat extremes, climate-model uncertainty, and how weather shapes power systems.

Research

My work uses observations, climate-model ensembles, statistical methods, and regional downscaling to understand climate variability and improve projections of regional change. I am especially interested in heat, hydroclimate, emergent constraints, and the separation of forced change from internal variability.

I am currently using the Western US Dynamically Downscaled Dataset (WUS-D3) with PyPSA to explore how climate and weather data can inform power-grid and renewable-energy simulations.

I work with UC Irvine's Climate, Pollution, and Solutions group.

Projects

Madera County Drought Dashboard

A public information site for Madera County residents whose domestic well has gone dry or is at risk. It leads with the agencies to contact, then sets local groundwater storage, drought category, rainfall, Sierra snowpack, and temperature against the official records they come from — each panel stating plainly what it does not mean.

California DWR, U.S. Drought Monitor, USDA NRCS, NOAA CPC · Python and vanilla JavaScript on Vercel

Roof (m²)

A web tool for outlining a roof on satellite imagery and estimating its solar-energy and rainwater-harvesting potential from location-specific climate data.

Next.js, TypeScript, Google Maps, NASA POWER · Source
