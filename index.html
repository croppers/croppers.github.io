Stephen Cropper

Postdoctoral researcher in Earth System Science at UC Irvine. I study regional climate dynamics, heat extremes, climate-model uncertainty, and how weather shapes power systems.

Email · GitHub · ORCID · Google Scholar

Research

My work uses observations, climate-model ensembles, statistical methods, and regional downscaling to understand climate variability and improve projections of regional change. I am especially interested in heat, hydroclimate, emergent constraints, and the separation of forced change from internal variability.

I work in the Climate, Pollution, and Solutions group, using the Western US Dynamically Downscaled Dataset (WUS-D3) with PyPSA to explore how climate and weather data can inform power-grid and renewable-energy simulations.

Projects

Madera County Drought Dashboard

A site for Madera County residents to use to learn about resources for addressing dry wells or emergency water needs. It also contains a dashboard to view recent trends in drought, groundwater storage, snowpack, rainfall, and temperatures against historical records.

California DWR, U.S. Drought Monitor, USDA NRCS, NOAA CPC · Python and vanilla JavaScript on Vercel

Roof (m²)

A web app that projects rooftop solar generation and rainwater capture based on satellite imagery. All you need is an address or GPS location!

Next.js, TypeScript, Google Maps, NASA POWER · Source
