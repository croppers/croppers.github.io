---
title: "Emergent Constraints: Bridging the Gap Between Models and Observations"
date: "2024-02-28"
description: "Stephen Cropper explains emergent constraints, their physical basis, and how observations can narrow uncertainty in climate projections."
---

Emergent constraints use relationships between present-day climate and future climate change across models to narrow projection uncertainty. The method is useful only when the relationship has a credible physical basis and remains robust across models and observations.

Important questions include:

- What physical mechanism creates the relationship?
- Is the relationship stable across model generations?
- Are the observations sufficiently precise and independent?
- Does model dependence make the apparent sample size misleading?

My work applies these questions to equilibrium climate sensitivity and regional climate change. The aim is not simply to find a strong regression, but to identify relationships that improve projections for defensible physical reasons.
