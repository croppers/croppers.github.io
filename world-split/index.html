How the live weight works

The baseline is VT’s latest published foreign-holdings weight, a practical proxy for the investable global stock market.

Each side is rolled forward using the dividend-reinvested return of VTI and VXUS, then normalized back to 100%.

In ETF shares mode, VT represents the total-world position being replaced. Current prices convert it into market-value-equivalent VTI and VXUS share counts, including fractional shares.

US weight = (US₀ × VTI total return) ÷ (US₀ × VTI + ex-US₀ × VXUS)
