The portfolio holds the same U.S. and international proportions as the investable global market. Within each region, your chosen tilt determines how much moves from the broad-market core into small-cap value.

The live geographic split begins with VT’s latest published foreign-holdings weight, then rolls each side forward using the dividend-reinvested return of VTI and VXUS.

Because AVUV and AVDV use the same tilt inside their respective regions, the factor allocation scales in lockstep with global market capitalizations instead of introducing a separate regional bet.

VTI = (1 − T) × WUS
AVUV = T × WUS
VXUS = (1 − T) × WIntl
AVDV = T × WIntl

In ETF shares mode, VT represents the total-world position being allocated. Live prices convert that market value into fractional shares of all four funds.
